# Privacy

Plain language, because you should know exactly what we keep.

**What we store**

- Your email address, and a first name if you gave one.
- The telemetry your devices send, for **90 days**, in your own tenant.
- An audit log of sign-ins and account actions (sign-up, device created, token rotated) for **30 days**, with the request's IP address.
- Which class code you signed up with, if any — so your instructor can see their roster.

**What we do not do**

- No third-party analytics, no advertising, no selling or sharing of data.
- No passwords stored by the portal: sign-in is handled by our identity service (Keycloak), and your device tokens are stored only in ThingsBoard, inside your tenant.

**Who can see what**

- You see everything in your tenant. Other students see nothing of yours.
- An instructor who issued your class code sees your email, your device count and message volume — not your data.
- Platform administrators can access tenants for support and abuse handling; every such access is logged.

**Age.** You must be 18 or older to sign up on your own. Younger students join only through a class code from their school, which is responsible for consent.

**Delete.** Ask us to delete your account and everything in your tenant is removed within 7 days. Contact: the address shown in the portal footer.
